CarbTab: the app, through the panels

The built app, not a mockup. The same eleven audience panels from the naming study were walked through the home screen, a search, the weigh sheet, the day log, settings, and the new folder-tab logo, then asked one question: would you keep this on your phone?

Verdict

5 Yes 6 Probably 0 Not yet

Nobody rejected it, which is the real signal: the core is right. The home screen is finally big and calm, the giant carb number in the weigh sheet is exactly what people want at a glance, and the source tag on every food earns trust. The six "probably" votes clustered on the same short list of fixable things, led by one that made several rooms say the app feels unfinished.

Punch list

Update, 2026-09-09. The three P0s are now shipped in the build. P1 and P2 remain open.
P0

The Photo and Scan tiles do nothingDone

Two of the three big buttons on the home screen show a "coming soon" toast. On a health tool, a dead button reads as "this isn't finished, what else doesn't work." Either build them or remove them for launch and replace the row with Restaurants and Add a food.

Fixed: the home screen now has two live tiles, Restaurants and Add a food.

P0

Whole foods are grams onlyDone

Most people do not own a gram scale, and "158 g of rice" is not a picture anyone has in their head. The data already carries a serving label and weight for every food ("1 cup, 158 g"), so offer that as a tappable unit and let people multiply it (2 slices, half a cup). Grams stays for the people who weigh.

Fixed: the weigh sheet has a By weight / By serving switch, with a half-to-three quantity picker and the gram total shown. It opens on By weight and remembers whichever you last used.

P0

The Net carbs toggle changes nothingDone

There is no fiber data yet, so switching to Net silently falls back to Total. A setting that does nothing when you use it is worse than no setting. Hide it until the fiber pass is done, or grey it out with "coming with the next data update."

Fixed: Net is greyed out and labelled "soon", and Settings now says the app counts total carbohydrate until the fiber pass lands.

P1

No memory of what you ate last time

People eat the same breakfast every day. Re-typing "40 g" of the same granola every morning is friction the app could remove: "you logged 40 g yesterday" as a one-tap option in the weigh sheet.

Veterans, parents, athletes

P1

The weight field pre-fills and gets in the way

It opens with the serving weight already typed in, so people who weigh have to clear it first. Select the value on focus, or start the field empty with the serving offered as a chip.

Veterans, mixed room

P1

"Unverified" on the restaurant seed undercuts confidence

The honesty is right, but a parent counting for a child sees "Unverified" on McDonald's fries and stops trusting the whole app. Ship a smaller set of actually-verified 2025 items rather than a large seed marked unverified, and reword the tag to say what verification is pending.

Parents, the clinic

P1

No undo after adding

A mis-tap inflates the day's total and the only fix is to go to the Log and delete it. Add an "Undo" to the confirmation toast.

Every room

P1

The 150 g daily goal is arbitrary and a little judgmental

Where 150 comes from is unclear, and "122 g left before the 150 g goal" reads as a scold to some people. Ask for the goal in onboarding, label it "set this with your care team," and keep the running text neutral.

Parents, low-carb counters, older adults

P2

Nothing to see before you search

First-timers do not know what is in the database. Show a few common foods or a category row on the home screen, not only in the Foods tab.

First ninety days, second-language, older adults

P2

Weigh-sheet ARIA is loose

The size toggle announces as tabs but there is no tab panel behind it, and the preset chips need a clearer spoken pressed state. Make it a plain group of toggle buttons or a real tablist.

Screen reader

P2

"tap to add" actually opens the weigh sheet

Small wording mismatch. "Tap to log" is truer, and for restaurant items consider a genuine one-tap add.

Mixed room

Keep doing

✓The giant carb number in the weigh sheet. Every room noticed it, several said it is the whole reason the app feels usable in a hurry.
✓A source tag on every food. USDA, or the restaurant name, or Custom. The clinicians and the privacy-minded users specifically called this out.
✓One column, bottom nav, everything thumb-reachable. The original "too small on mobile" complaint is gone.
✓Offline, no account, no ads, free. Said back to us unprompted in four rooms.
✓Total carbs by default, and the "not a medical device, does not tell you your insulin" line up front.
✓The disclaimer shown once, then a quiet footer line. The anxious rooms found it reassuring rather than alarming.

On the logo

The folder-tab mark landed without friction. Rooms read it as "a tab, a little list," which is the point, and it reads at small sizes. The designer voice in the mixed room: "clean, the two green lines are a nice touch, maybe a touch safe but it is a real mark." The teen room: "a bit corporate, not bad." Nobody disliked it. Fine to ship, room to evolve later.

The eleven panels

01

A mixed room

Probably

Seven people across age, diabetes type and tech comfort.

"Cleaner than anything I have tried. Search a thing, get a number, done. But a camera button that just says coming makes me wonder what else is half built."

AskHide the unbuilt tiles, or build them.
02

The first ninety days

Yes

Four adults three weeks to three months past a Type 1 diagnosis.

"It did not overwhelm me. The big number is reassuring, it feels like the app is sure. I would want a few foods to tap right away though, I do not know what to search for yet."

AskStarter foods on the home screen.
03

Counting for someone small

Probably

Three parents of Type 1 kids, plus a grandparent on pickup duty.

"The handoff works, I can tell the sitter to open CarbTab. Two problems: I can not set Ellie's 45 g lunch target properly, and Unverified on the McDonald's items means I go check the real number anyway, so what did the app save me?"

AskVerified restaurant data, and a real goal setting.
04

Twenty years in

Yes

Four people with Type 1 for 18 to 34 years, all pump plus CGM.

"Finally, one that is just fast and does not try to be my friend. Let me type the weight without deleting the number that is already there, and remember that I eat 40 g of the same granola every single day."

AskRepeat-last portion. Cleaner weight entry.
05

New to insulin, later in life

Probably

Four adults 61 to 74 with Type 2, all started insulin this year.

"Big enough to read without my glasses, thank you for that. But I do not own a gram scale and I could not tell you what 158 grams of anything looks like. Give me cups, or slices, or small and large."

AskHousehold portions, not just grams.
06

The clinic

Yes

Five clinicians deciding whether to recommend it to patients.

"The source tags and the not a medical device line are exactly right, I would put this on a handout. But turn the Net toggle off until it does something. A control that silently does nothing is how patients lose trust in a tool."

AskDisable Net until fiber data exists.
07

Nine months, then done

Yes

Four people managing gestational diabetes, weeks 24 to 36.

"Low commitment, does one thing, I will use it until August and delete it, and that is fine. The grams thing is annoying without a scale, I have been guessing."

AskNon-scale portions.
08

English as a second language

Yes

Five adults whose first languages are Spanish, Tagalog, Arabic, Mandarin, Portuguese.

"Simple words, big buttons. The little USDA label is a picture I understand without reading. Easy to use. Later, other languages would help."

AskNothing blocking. Localisation eventually.
09

The group chat

Probably

Four people with Type 1, ages 16 to 23.

"It is clean, I would use it and I would recommend it. But the camera icon that just says coming soon looks unfinished, and first impressions are everything when someone opens an app you sent them."

AskDo not ship visible dead features.
10

Carbs for performance

Probably

Four active people with Type 1, two runners, a cyclist, a lifter.

"Good for logging after. Mid-ride I fuel by the gram and I want to log three gels, 66 g in two taps without searching each one. Right now that is a lot of tapping."

AskFast repeat and quick custom entry.
11

Reads it aloud

Probably

Four people who navigate by screen reader or large text, plus one with a tremor.

"Reads mostly well and the targets are big, which helps the tremor. In the weigh sheet the size switch announces as tabs but there is nothing behind them, and the preset buttons do not clearly say which one is chosen."

AskTighten the weigh-sheet ARIA.
The app reviewed is the build in CarbTab/app/ at commit-time 2026-09-09: Home, weigh sheet, Log, Foods, Settings, first-run disclaimer, folder-tab logo.
